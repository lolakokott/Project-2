## 6. Final Investment Decision

**PILOT, not immediate adoption.**

The constrained portfolio satisfies the mandate under the tested historical assumptions and demonstrates lower estimated volatility than the two baselines. However, sensitivity to return assumptions, estimation windows, annual costs, and liquidity requirements supports a monitored pilot rather than unconditional implementation.

The process should be reassessed or suspended if the portfolio fails its mandate, no feasible allocation exists, material data limitations cannot be resolved, or allocation changes exceed the committee's approved tolerance.
